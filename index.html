Video Game History

Video game analysis involves the systematic examination of a game's design, mechanics, narrative, and user experience to understand its impact and effectiveness. Through this process, designers and critics evaluate factors like engagement, balance, and aesthetic appeal to deconstruct what makes a title successful or unsuccessful. This critical review helps inform future development practices and enhances the broader academic understanding of interactive media as an art form and a technological product.
